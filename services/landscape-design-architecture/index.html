Architectural Outdoor Planning

Landscape Design & Architecture

Transforming residential gardens, luxury villa grounds, terrace balconies, and commercial properties into enduring architectural landscapes.

What is Landscape Design & Architecture at THE ERA?

THE ERA landscape design and architecture service encompasses comprehensive spatial planning, site assessment, bespoke garden layout, botanical selection, hardscape integration (pathways, stone elements, water features), and structural execution planning tailored to luxury living environments.

What We Design

Our architectural design approach balances aesthetic luxury with environmental sustainability:

Villa & Residential Gardens

Expansive lawn layouts, private courtyard gardens, flowerbed compositions, and privacy hedge architecture.

Terrace & Balcony Gardens

Optimized container arrangements, vertical foliage framing, wind-tolerant plant placement, and outdoor seating landscapes.

Hardscape Integration & Pathways

Architectural stone paths, decorative gravel, lawn edging, wooden deck integration, and water feature alignment.

Commercial Landscapes

Refined corporate entrance greening, courtyard installations, and low-maintenance ornamental grounds.

Our 7-Stage Design & Execution Process

A structured, transparent workflow ensuring meticulous execution from initial consultation to long-term landscape maturity:

Step 01

Consultation

In-depth discussion of client preferences, lifestyle requirements, spatial goals, and architectural theme.

Step 02

Site Assessment

Evaluating sunlight patterns, soil conditions, drainage, wind exposure, and existing structural elements.

Step 03

Concept & Layout

Developing spatial concepts, zoning outdoor areas, pathway flow, and defining hardscape/softscape proportions.

Step 04

Planting Strategy

Selecting foliage, trees, turfgrass (such as Korean Grass), and flowering plants matched to microclimates.

Step 05

Landscape Design

Refining detailed planting plans, irrigation layouts, lighting placement, and material specifications.

Step 06

Execution Planning

Coordinating planting schedules, soil enrichment, turf installation, and precision garden building.

Step 07

Maintenance Planning

Providing custom care protocols and offering ongoing Professional Gardening Services.
