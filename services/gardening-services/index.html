Professional Gardening Services

Preserving the vitality, structure, and aesthetic beauty of residential gardens, villa lawns, and commercial outdoor spaces.

What do THE ERA Professional Gardening Services Include?

THE ERA gardening services include comprehensive garden maintenance, lawn mowing and edging, precision tree & hedge pruning, plant replacement, weed management, seasonal cleanup, soil aeration, and indoor/outdoor plant health management.

Gardening Services Offered

Our skilled gardening specialists deliver meticulous care tailored to your garden's specific botanical requirements:

Routine Garden Maintenance

Scheduled visits for weeding, deadheading, mulching, watering inspection, and general garden tidy-up.

Lawn Care & Mowing

Turf trimming, precision edging, aeration, dethatching, and maintenance for Korean Grass and natural lawns.

Pruning & Hedge Shaping

Architectural trimming of ornamental bushes, structural hedge shaping, and tree canopy pruning for optimal light penetration.

Plant Installation & Refresh

Planting new seasonal blooms, replacing mature foliage, repotting container plants, and soil enrichment.
