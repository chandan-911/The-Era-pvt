Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need
to know about THE ERA.

Discover how we design, nurture, deliver, and transform spaces into living green environments.

What services does THE ERA offer?
THE ERA provides four primary services: 1) Landscape Design & Architecture for residential, villa, and commercial spaces; 2) Professional Gardening Services including garden setup and ongoing maintenance; 3) Plant Delivery for indoor and outdoor curated plants; and 4) Plant Care & Fertilizer Recommendations.
Who is THE ERA designed for?
THE ERA is designed for homeowners, luxury villa owners, balcony & terrace garden enthusiasts, and commercial properties looking for refined, professionally designed, and well-maintained green spaces.
What types of lawn grass does THE ERA provide?
THE ERA provides technical guidance and installation recommendations for premium lawn turf varieties including Korean Grass (Zoysia japonica) and Selection Grass. Learn more on our Lawn Grass Guide page.
How can I request a landscape design consultation or plant care advice?
You can connect directly with THE ERA team on WhatsApp (+91 70094 46683) or submit an inquiry through our Contact Page.
