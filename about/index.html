About THE ERA

Art of Living Green.

THE ERA is a premium landscape architecture, professional gardening, curated plant, and plant-care solutions provider dedicated to architectural living spaces.

Who is THE ERA?

THE ERA is a professional green-lifestyle and landscape architecture brand. We specialize in bespoke outdoor spatial planning, villa garden installation, routine gardening maintenance, curated indoor and outdoor plant delivery, and expert plant nutrition recommendations.

Brand Philosophy

Operating under the guiding philosophy "Art of Living Green", THE ERA elevates residential and commercial environments through four structured pillars:

Pillar 01

DESIGN

Bespoke landscape architecture, spatial planning, hardscape integration, and aesthetic outdoor concepts tailored to villas and modern architectural properties.

Pillar 02

NURTURE

Professional gardening maintenance, lawn care, tree pruning, soil enrichment, and seasonal plant monitoring.

Pillar 03

DELIVER

White-glove delivery of hand-picked indoor foliage, outdoor ornamental specimens, specialized lawn grasses, and planters.

Pillar 04

TRANSFORM

Elevating living environments into enduring, vibrant green spaces that harmonize nature with modern living.

Our Service Scope

THE ERA delivers solutions across key landscape disciplines:

  • Landscape Design & Architecture — Spatial layout, villa gardens, terrace & balcony greening.
  • Professional Gardening Services — Maintenance programs, lawn installation, pruning, cleanup.
  • Curated Plant Delivery — Indoor air-purifiers, outdoor flora, container trees.
  • Plant Care & Nutrition — Organic fertilizer selection, plant diagnostics, soil care.
