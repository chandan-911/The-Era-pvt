Spatial Garden Planning

Garden Design & Architecture

Creating harmonious outdoor spaces that blend architectural minimalism with lush botanical foliage for modern homes, villas, and terraces.

What does Garden Design at THE ERA involve?

THE ERA garden design service blends spatial zoning, plant selection, lighting, water elements, lawn integration (including Korean Grass), and hardscape paths into a unified, low-maintenance outdoor sanctuary.

Garden Design Pillars

Pillar 01

Villa & Residential Gardens

Bespoke garden planning for luxury private homes, incorporating manicured turf, specimen trees, and private courtyards.

Pillar 02

Terrace & Balcony Gardens

Container spatial layouts, vertical green framing, wind-tolerant foliage, and outdoor seating landscapes.

Pillar 03

Low-Maintenance Landscapes

Designing sustainable gardens with automatic irrigation planning, hardy foliage, and weed-suppressing turf cover.
