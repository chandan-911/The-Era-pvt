Botanical Care Guide

Areca Palm (Dypsis lutescens)

An elegant tropical clumping palm featuring soft, arching fronds widely appreciated as an air-purifying indoor and balcony container plant.

How to Care for an Areca Palm?

Place the Areca Palm in bright, indirect sunlight, keep the soil consistently moist but never waterlogged, use well-draining pot soil enriched with organic compost, maintain moderate humidity, and feed with balanced organic fertilizer during spring and summer.

Care Instructions & Troubleshooting

Light & Location

Sunlight Position

Thrives near bright East or South-facing windows behind sheer curtains. Avoid direct afternoon scorching sun which turns leaves yellow.

Watering & Soil

Moisture Management

Water when the top inch of soil feels dry to the touch. Ensure container has adequate drainage holes to prevent root rot.

Troubleshooting

Brown Leaf Tips

Brown tips indicate low ambient humidity, underwatering, or fluoride buildup from tap water. Mist fronds regularly.
