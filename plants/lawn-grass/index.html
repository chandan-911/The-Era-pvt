Lawn Grass Guide

Understanding the characteristics, sun/water requirements, and maintenance considerations for natural lawn grass varieties.

Which Lawn Grass is Best for Your Landscape?

The choice between lawn grass varieties depends on foot traffic, sunlight exposure, and aesthetic preferences. Korean Grass provides a soft, fine-textured velvet carpet ideal for manicured villa lawns, while Selection Grass offers dense, high-durability turf suitable for active gardens.

Factual Comparison: Korean Grass vs. Selection Grass

Korean Grass (Zoysia japonica)

Texture: Fine, velvet-like carpet blades.

Ideal Use: Luxury residential villas, courtyards, ornamental lawns.

Maintenance: Regular precision trimming and deep watering.

Explore Korean Grass →

Selection Grass

Texture: Medium-wide, dense, resilient turf.

Ideal Use: Family gardens, active lawns, commercial green grounds.

Maintenance: Routine mowing and general lawn watering.
