Specialty Turf Variety

Korean Grass (Zoysia japonica)

A fine-textured, dense perennial turfgrass renowned for forming a soft, carpet-like lawn ideal for luxury villa grounds and architectural gardens.

What is Korean Grass?

Korean Grass (botanical name: Zoysia japonica) is a warm-season turfgrass known for its dense, needle-fine leaf blade, soft velvety texture, strong root system, moderate drought tolerance, and elegant carpet-like appearance in residential lawns.

Key Advantages of Korean Grass

  • Luxurious Velvet Texture: Creates a dense, cushioned green carpet that feels comfortable underfoot.
  • Durable Root Network: Spreads stoloniferously to form a thick turf layer that naturally suppresses weeds once established.
  • Subtle Shadowing & Rich Color: Maintains a vibrant deep-emerald hue under full sunlight conditions.
  • Ideal for Architectural Lawns: Perfect for manicured residential courtyards, villa gardens, and high-end residential landscaping.

Installation & Care Recommendations

Successful Korean Grass establishment requires well-drained sandy loam soil enriched with organic matter. For complete care details, visit our Korean Grass Care & Maintenance Guide.
