Korean Grass Care & Landscaping Guide

Published by THE ERA Botanical Specialists · Updated September 2026

Summary: How to Maintain Korean Grass Lawns

Korean Grass (Zoysia japonica) requires full sunlight, well-draining sandy loam soil, deep morning watering 2 to 3 times per week, maintaining a mowing height of 1.0 to 1.5 inches with sharp mower blades, and light organic nitrogen fertilization during active growing seasons.

1. Soil Preparation & Installation

Establishing a healthy Korean Grass lawn starts with proper soil foundation. Remove existing weeds and stones, grade the site for 1-2% slope to prevent standing water, and incorporate organic compost into sandy loam soil. Ensure soil pH is between 6.0 and 7.0 for optimal root nutrient uptake.

2. Irrigation & Moisture Management

Water deeply in the early morning hours (between 5:00 AM and 8:00 AM) to allow leaf moisture to dry naturally under sunlight. Avoid light, frequent surface watering which leads to shallow root growth. Established Korean Grass benefits from 1 inch of water per week split across 2 to 3 sessions.

3. Mowing Height & Frequency

Maintain Korean Grass at a height of 1.0 to 1.5 inches (2.5 to 3.8 cm). Never remove more than one-third of the grass blade in a single mowing session. Sharp mower blades are essential to make clean cuts; dull blades tear the fine leaves, creating yellowed tip edges.

4. Fertilizing & Lawn Nutrition

Apply balanced organic fertilizer during warm active growth periods. Avoid heavy nitrogen applications during cold dormant seasons. For tailored fertilizer recommendations for your garden, consult our Plant Care & Fertilizer Recommendations page.
